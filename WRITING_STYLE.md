# Writing Style Guide

## Essay Preferences

| Aspect | Choice | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Tone** | Journalistic | Clear, engaging, like The Economist or NYT |
| **Audience** | Curious learners | Interested readers willing to engage with concepts |
| **Structure** | First principles | Build up from basics, show the "why" behind concepts |
| **Depth** | Comprehensive | 20-30 min read, full exploration with nuance and caveats |

## Guidelines

### Voice
- Third person or direct address ("you") where it aids clarity
- Avoid jargon; when technical terms are necessary, define them
- Active voice preferred

### Structure Pattern
1. **Hook**: Why should the reader care?
2. **Foundation**: Core concepts from first principles
3. **Build-up**: Layer complexity progressively
4. **Evidence**: Data and charts support the narrative
5. **Nuance**: Address limitations, alternatives, caveats
6. **Synthesis**: What does this mean for the reader?

### Chart Integration
- Charts should advance the argument, not just illustrate
- Every chart needs a clear "so what?" insight
- Captions should be informative enough to stand alone

### The Economist Style Notes
- Short paragraphs (3-5 sentences)
- One idea per paragraph
- Lead with the most interesting point
- Use concrete examples over abstractions
- Numbers should be contextualized ("roughly the size of France's economy")
