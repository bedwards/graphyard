## What Is GDP?

<Definition term="Gross Domestic Product" pronunciation="GDP">
The total monetary value of all final goods and services produced within a
country's borders during a specific time period, typically one year or quarter.
</Definition>

{/*
Core concepts to cover:
- "Gross" vs "Net" (depreciation)
- "Domestic" vs "National" (geographic vs citizenship)
- "Product" - output, not income (though they're equivalent)
- "Final" goods - avoiding double counting
*/}

## The Three Approaches to Measuring GDP

{/*
The GDP identity: all three must equal the same number.
*/}

### Production Approach (Value Added)

{/*
- Sum of value added at each stage
- Avoids double-counting
*/}

### Expenditure Approach

{/*
Y = C + I + G + (X - M)

- Consumption (C): Household spending
- Investment (I): Business capital, housing, inventories
- Government (G): Government purchases (not transfers)
- Net Exports (X - M): Trade balance
*/}
