## Military Spending Data

### SIPRI Military Expenditure Database
- **URL**: https://www.sipri.org/databases/milex
- **Coverage**: 1949-2024
- **Format**: Downloadable Excel/CSV
- **Key finding**: World military spending reached $2.7 trillion in 2024
- **US share**: Nearly 40% of global military spending
- **Methodology**: Standardized definitions, primary government sources

### Historical Military Spending (Pre-1949)
- **Source**: EH.net Encyclopedia
- **Key finding**: UK during Napoleonic Wars spent £1.65 billion (1793-1815)
  - 75% from taxation, 25% from loans
  - Army grew from 57,000 to 255,000
  - Navy from 16,000 to 140,000
- **Source**: Statista UK Historical Military Expenditure
  - Coverage: 1688-1968
  - Major spikes: Napoleonic Wars, WWI, WWII

### US Taxpayer Military Contribution
- **Source**: National Priorities Project (2023)
- **Key figure**: $5,109 per taxpayer for militarism
  - Includes Pentagon, veterans, border, policing
  - $1,748 to Pentagon contractors alone
- **Context**: More than average monthly rent ($1,372)
- **63 days**: Work days to fund military spending

## Conflict Death Data

### UCDP (Uppsala Conflict Data Program)
- **URL**: https://ucdp.uu.se/
- **Definition**: Armed conflict = 25+ battle deaths/year
- **Coverage**: 1946-present (GED: 1989-present)
- **2024 finding**: 204,605 conflict events, 240,000+ deaths
- **Civilian targeting**: Up from 20% (2020) to 35% (2025) by state forces

### Rummel Democide Data
- **URL**: hawaii.edu/powerkills
- **Definition**: Death by government (excluding legitimate war)
- **20th century total**: 262 million
- **Top perpetrators**:
  - Soviet Union: 62 million
  - Communist China: 35 million
  - Nazi Germany: 21 million
- **Methodology**: 8,200 estimates from 1,000+ sources
- **Criticism**: Communist figures contested; not updated post-1999
- **Free download**: Full books available as PDFs

### Brown University Costs of War
- **URL**: watson.brown.edu/costsofwar
- **Post-9/11 findings**:
  - 940,000+ direct deaths (2001-2023)
  - 432,000+ civilians
  - 3.6-3.7 million indirect deaths
  - 38+ million displaced
- **Indirect death ratio**: ~4:1 vs. direct deaths
- **Iraq**: 185,000-208,000 violent deaths documented
- **Afghanistan**: 176,000+ total deaths

### Iraq Body Count
- **URL**: iraqbodycount.org
- **Method**: Documented civilian deaths from media/NGO reports
- **Total**: 51,608 incidents documented
- **Known conservative**: Captures only documented deaths

## Historical Income Data

### Maddison Project Database
- **URL**: rug.nl/ggdc/historicaldevelopment/maddison
- **Coverage**: 1 CE - 2023 (1800-2023 more complete)
- **Format**: 2011 international dollars (PPP)
- **Latest version**: 2023 update
- **Key finding**: US overtook UK in GDP/capita in 1870s (not early 1900s as traditionally thought)

### US Census Historical Income
- **URL**: census.gov/data/tables/time-series/demo/income-poverty/historical-income-households.html
- **Coverage**: 1967-present (continuous)
- **2024 finding**: Median household income $83,730
- **Inflation adjustment**: C-CPI-U (2000-2024), R-CPI-U-RS (pre-2000)

### FRED Data Series
- **MEHOINUSA672N**: Real Median Household Income
- **MEPAINUSA672N**: Real Median Personal Income
- **Coverage**: 1974-2024

### Lindert-Williamson Research
- **Coverage**: 1650-1860 American incomes
- **Key finding**: Colonists had higher income than English in 1774
- **Decline**: Real per capita income fell 1774-1800

## Tax Burden Data

### Historical Tax Rates
- **Source**: Tax Foundation, Bradford Tax Institute
- **1980**: Top marginal rate 70%, top 1% paid 19% of income taxes
- **1990s**: Top rate 39.6%
- **2022**: Top 1% paid 40.4% of income taxes
- **Bottom 50%**: Share dropped from 7% (1980) to 2.96% (2022)

### CBO Distribution Data
- **Coverage**: 1979-present
- **Peak average rate**: 16.1% (1981)
- **Peak effective rate**: 22.7% (2000)
- **Post-2001**: Sharp decline from tax cuts

### IRS Statistics of Income
- **URL**: irs.gov/statistics
- **Coverage**: 1958-present (detailed), 1913-present (summary)
- **1979 Income Concept**: Allows consistent historical comparison

## Rwanda Case Study

### Weapons and Costs
- **Arms imports 1990-1993**: $112 million
- **Main weapons**: Machetes (agricultural tools), small arms
- **Deaths**: 800,000-1 million in 100 days
- **Implied cost per death**: ~$140

### Key Sources
- **Foreign Affairs**: "Arming Genocide in Rwanda"
- **Journal of Peace Research**: Verwimp (2006) on machetes vs. firearms
- **Economic analysis**: One of world's poorest countries went bankrupt buying weapons

### Macroeconomic Impact
- **GDP decline 1994**: 58%
- **Recovery**: 17 years to match counterfactual
- **Population adjusted**: 31% decline in GDP per capita

## Philosophical Framework

### Just War Theory
- **Origin**: Augustine, Aquinas, developed by Grotius, Vattel
- **Key principles**: Jus ad bellum (going to war), jus in bello (conduct in war)
- **Core claim**: Some wars morally justified; killing combatants permissible
- **Civilian protection**: Non-combatants may never be targeted

### Pacifism
- **Absolute pacifism**: All violence wrong, no exceptions
- **Contingent pacifism**: War theoretically possible but never justified in practice
- **War tax resistance**: Extension of conscientious objection to taxation
- **Historical figures**: Woolman (1755), Thoreau (1846), Peacemakers (1948)

### Legal Framework
- **US Peace Tax Fund**: Proposed since 1972, never passed
- **Welsh v. United States (1970)**: Expanded CO status to non-theistic objectors
- **Key challenge**: Precedent for opting out of any government spending

## Indirect Deaths Methodology

### Definitions
- **Direct**: Violence (bombs, bullets, execution)
- **Indirect**: Disease, starvation, displacement consequences
- **Ratnayake typology**: Baseline mortality + excess mortality (direct + indirect)

### Ratios
- **Modern conflicts**: ~4:1 indirect to direct (Brown Costs of War)
- **DRC, Darfur**: <20% of child deaths from direct trauma
- **Leading causes**: Fever/malaria, measles, diarrhea, respiratory infections

### Measurement Challenges
- **Only 18.6%** of conflict mortality studies report crude death rates
- **Only 7%** report indirect deaths separately
- **No studies** provided definitions for conflict-related deaths
